--- bug found: the ratio gate was poisoned by the floating pads ---
The gate asks "is this excursion frontally dominant?" by comparing frontal
deviation against the posterior mean. With six posterior pads floating at ten
times the frontal amplitude, every genuine blink scored a ratio below 1:

  with gate as written:   2 blink-type events, 13 rejected by ratio
  with gate disabled:     7 blink-type events,  0 rejected

Fixed in detect.py: the posterior reference set is narrowed at calibration to
pads that pass contact QC, and if none qualify the gate reports
  "ratio gate DISABLED: no usable posterior reference (O1,O2,C3,CZ,FZ,C4 all
   failed contact QC)"
and stops gating rather than gating on noise. Regression test [4b] covers both
directions: floating pads dropped, clean pads kept.

Note this weakens artifact rejection whenever the posterior pads are bad, which
is a further reason to fix them - the gate is the main defence against cable
sway and electrode pops being read as gestures.
